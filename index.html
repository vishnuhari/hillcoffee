Quick Guide: Arabica vs Robusta

Understanding the bean characteristics of Idukki terroir

Arabica
  • Taste Profile: Smoother, more aromatic and a little brighter
  • Acidity & Body: Gentle acidity, delicate refined body
  • Caffeine: Lower caffeine, clean finish
  • Best For: Black coffee, pour-overs, cold brew, and gentle extraction
Robusta
  • Taste Profile: Stronger, bolder and fuller in body
  • Acidity & Body: Low acidity, dense crema and thick mouthfeel
  • Caffeine: Nearly double the caffeine punch
  • Best For: Milk coffee, South Indian filter, and intense espresso
THE HILLS NOTE

Not a budget option — good Robusta, like Idukki Robusta, is a different, richer cup. Grown at high elevations under shade alongside pepper and spices, it carries a protected GI (Geographical Indication) tag and commands pride of place.

THE IDUKKI TERROIR

High Mountains. Shade-Grown With Pepper & Cardamom.

Nestled along the verdant ridges of Kerala's Western Ghats, Idukki's coffee farms thrive within a polyculture ecosystem. Here, Arabica and Robusta shrubs flourish beneath dense canopy trees alongside cardamom pods, black pepper vines, and arecanut palms.

This distinctive shade-grown microclimate allows coffee cherries to ripen slowly, absorbing rich volcanic minerality and organic spice notes that you won't find anywhere else on earth.

EXTRACTION ESSENTIALS

Brew It Like The Hills

Master your brew with our tested ratios for Idukki specialty roasts.

Recommended for Robusta & Blend

South Indian Filter

Ratio 1:5 Decoction
Time 15-20 Mins
Grind Fine-Medium

Add 20g ground coffee into upper chamber. Tamp lightly with the pressing disc. Pour 100ml hot water (92°C). Let thick aromatic decoction slowly drip. Mix 1:3 with hot frothy whole milk.

Recommended for Arabica

Pour-Over / Dripper

Ratio 1:15 Coffee:Water
Time 3:00 Mins
Grind Medium

Bloom 18g coffee with 45ml water for 45s to release fresh floral and chocolate aromas. Pour remaining 225ml in gentle concentric rings. Enjoy clean, spice-accented black cup.

Recommended for All 3

French Press Plunger

Ratio 1:14 Rich Body
Time 4:30 Mins
Grind Coarse

Add 25g coarse grind to carafe. Pour 350ml hot water, stir gently after 1 minute. Let steep for 4 minutes total. Slowly plunge and pour immediately to prevent bitterness.
